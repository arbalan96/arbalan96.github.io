# About me
----------

Hi! I am a post-doctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Software Systems (MPI-SWS), working with Rupak Majumdar. I received my Ph.D. *summa cum laude* from the Technical University of Munich in 2024, supervised by  Javier Esparza. From October 2024 to March 2025 I held a Vertretungsprofessur (interim professorship) at the Technical University of Munich.

My research concerns the **algorithmic analysis of infinite-state systems** and its applications to formal verification. I approach such analysis problems through a mixture of
* Developing faster algorithms and techniques for upper bounds
* Proving matching lower bounds
* Coming up with tractable approximations when exact analysis becomes infeasible

More recently, I am also working on applying these techniques to domains such as substructural logics, functional synthesis and planning in symbolic AI.
